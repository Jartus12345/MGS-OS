# MGS Output Specification v1.0

## OUTPUT A — MGS MASTER CLIENT STRATEGY (LAYER 2)
The detailed strategic source of truth. Contains: business context; independent source findings; cross-analysis (strategically relevant findings only — not the full Layer 1 evidence register); strategic findings; central diagnosis; current vs desired position; perception gap; strategic opportunity; North Star; strategic priorities; 12-month perception goal; Build/Protect/Reduce; strategic pillars; Align/Execute/Improve roadmap; supporting evidence and reasoning. No fixed page count — depth follows evidence. Reads as professional strategic analysis, not as an exposed evidence dossier — see the Client-Facing Writing Discipline in the Evidence Weighting & Layers component.

## OUTPUT B — CLIENT EXECUTIVE BRAND DIRECTION (LAYER 3)
Approximately 5–8 sections, for senior leadership approval — the same reasoning as the Master Strategy at executive depth, never a separate analysis. Locked structure:
1. Purpose & Context — why the Discovery exercise was undertaken, what evidence informed it, what this document establishes.
2. Where the Organisation Is Today — a concise synthesis of current-position findings from leadership, employees, external assessment and company context; not a reproduction of the full research.
3. What the Discovery Tells Us — the major cross-analysis conclusions and strategic diagnosis: the most important patterns, the central perception gap, where substance is invisible externally, where ambition is ahead of current perception, and any major issue materially affecting direction.
4. Where the Organisation Needs to Go — Desired Position, Strategic North Star, central perception movement, major Strategic Priorities.
5. The 12-Month Direction — 12-Month Perception Goal, Build / Protect / Reduce, Strategic Pillars where appropriate.
6. How We Get There — the high-level Align → Execute → Improve process, without overwhelming senior leadership with execution detail.
7. Approval & Next Steps — what leadership is being asked to approve, what feedback is required, what approval unlocks, what MGS does next.

This must be a genuine executive synthesis, not a truncation of the Master Strategy and not a re-exposure of Layer 1 analysis. No major conclusion should appear here that does not exist in the Master Strategy. Retain the most important strategic substance while removing unnecessary analytical depth: Master Strategy = strategic depth + evidence + reasoning + implications; Executive Summary = diagnosis + direction + confidence + decision.

## INTERNAL MGS OUTPUTS — LAYER 1 (preserved but not automatically client-facing)
Discovery Completeness; Evidence Register; Source Analysis; Theme Matrix; Agreements; Contradiction Register; Perception Gaps; Hidden Strengths; Unsupported Ambitions; Underused Evidence; Current vs Desired Matrix; Strategic Findings; Evidence Gaps; Alignment Questions; Claim Audit; Genericness Audit; Reference Contamination Audit; Weighting Audit. This is the Internal Evidence & Analysis Dossier — available whenever MGS wants to inspect the reasoning behind Layer 2 and Layer 3, but it is not the principal client output.
