# MGS System Correction & Recalibration v1.1 — Evidence Weighting and the Three-Layer System

This component was added after the PDMS validation run exposed a misunderstanding: the pipeline was placing too much emphasis on exposing its own analytical machinery and not enough on using that machinery to produce the actual MGS strategic output. It governs every stage from Strategic Findings onward and overrides any earlier instinct to let raw evidence frequency dictate the strategy.

## THE FUNDAMENTAL CORRECTION

The methodology (extraction, classification, independent analysis, agreements, contradictions, perception gaps, hidden strengths, unsupported ambitions, evidence gaps, claim auditing, traceability) is the INTERNAL REASONING ENGINE. It is not the principal client output. It exists to improve the strategy — it must never replace the strategy. The pipeline does not stop after proving the evidence has been analysed correctly; it must go on to make commercially relevant strategic judgements and populate the locked Master Strategy framework.

## EVIDENCE STRENGTH ≠ STRATEGIC SIGNIFICANCE

These are not the same thing, and every downstream stage must keep them separate. Something may appear repeatedly in employee research and therefore have strong evidence behind it without deserving to define the commercial strategy. Something may be strategically central to leadership with limited supporting evidence today — that does not mean it should be discarded; it may be a desired future position that needs to be built and substantiated, not abandoned for lack of proof yet.

## THE FOUR SOURCES PLAY DIFFERENT STRATEGIC ROLES

- **Strategic Business Discovery / Senior Leadership** — establishes the desired commercial destination: where leadership is trying to take the organisation, what it wants to become known for, which opportunities matter commercially, what future position the strategy needs to support. This is the primary source of DIRECTION.
- **Employee Brand Survey** — establishes internal reality: what appears to exist inside the organisation, including culture, expertise, strengths, weaknesses, internal perceptions, organisational substance, and evidence that supports, challenges or qualifies leadership's ambition. It is a lens ON the direction, not a competing direction.
- **Digital Credibility Scorecard** — establishes current external visibility and perception: what an independent outsider can currently see, understand and believe. It measures the gap, it does not set the destination.
- **Company Context** — establishes factual business context and current self-presentation: what the organisation currently says about itself.
- **Cross-analysis** answers one question with all four: what is the most credible strategic route between where the organisation is today and where leadership wants it to go?

Employee findings and external observations can challenge, qualify or expose weaknesses in leadership's ambition. They must not casually replace the commercial direction with a different strategy simply because a theme recurs. Equally, leadership ambition must never be treated as established reality on its own say-so. The system's job is to find the credible route between ambition and reality — not to crown whichever source produced the most observations.

## FOUR TESTS FOR EVERY CANDIDATE STRATEGIC FINDING

Score every candidate finding against all four before it is allowed to shape the diagnosis, North Star, priorities or pillars:

1. **Evidence strength** — how well supported is this finding?
2. **Commercial significance** — how directly does it relate to leadership's stated business objectives and desired future direction?
3. **Perception significance** — how materially does it affect movement between current and desired perception?
4. **MGS relevance** — can brand, marketing and communications meaningfully influence it?

High evidence strength alone must NOT automatically make something a core strategic priority. The strongest findings combine several of these characteristics — a finding that is only strongly evidenced (frequent in one source) but scores low on commercial and perception significance belongs in the internal dossier as supporting texture, not in the Master Strategy's central diagnosis or North Star.

**Illustrative miscalibration to avoid** (a documented failure mode, not a template to reuse for any specific client): elevating a handful of individual observations about a narrow operational theme — noticed repeatedly in employee research — into the central positioning recommendation, when the stronger, better-supported commercial direction is what leadership itself has described at length and in detail. Such observations remain useful supporting evidence; they are not automatically the positioning. Likewise, a real and painful internal event (for instance, redundancies or a difficult organisational change) must be acknowledged as evidence about internal confidence and the need for the new direction to be credible internally — never hidden — but weighted proportionately against the wider commercial evidence rather than becoming one of the principal determinants of the entire strategy. The lesson to take from any such case is how MGS weighs evidence by strategic significance — never the specific conclusions themselves, which belong to that one client's evidence and transfer to no one else.

## THE THREE-LAYER SYSTEM

**Layer 1 — Internal Evidence & Analysis Dossier.** The analytical machinery: Discovery Completeness, Evidence Register, independent source analysis, evidence classification, Theme Matrix, Agreements, Contradictions, Perception Gaps, Hidden Strengths, Unsupported Ambitions, Underused Evidence, Current vs Desired Matrix, Evidence Gaps, Alignment Questions, Claim Audit, Genericness Audit, Reference Contamination Audit, evidence traceability. This is primarily an MGS internal working layer, available whenever MGS wants to inspect the reasoning. It is NOT normally the principal strategy document, and none of its internal labels (FACT, LEADERSHIP INTENT, INTERNAL PERCEPTION, EXTERNAL OBSERVATION, STRONG/MODERATE/EMERGING/UNSUPPORTED evidence strength) should be exposed in client-facing output unless there is a specific strategic reason to surface one.

**Layer 2 — MGS Master Client Brand Direction & Strategy.** The complete strategic source of truth. Takes Layer 1's analysis and turns it into coherent, commercially relevant strategic thinking using the locked MGS Client Strategy Framework (component1-framework.md / component6-output-spec.md).

**Layer 3 — MGS Executive Brand Direction Summary.** The senior-leadership expression of Layer 2 — what a CEO, Managing Director or Senior Leadership Team needs to understand → challenge → align → approve. No major conclusion appears in Layer 3 that does not exist in Layer 2, and Layer 3 does not reproduce Layer 2's full reasoning.

These three layers represent the SAME strategic reasoning at different depths. They must never become three separate analyses.

## STANDARDISATION PRINCIPLE

The methodology, the Master Strategy architecture and the Executive Summary architecture are consistent across every client. The evidence, diagnosis, North Star, priorities, pillars, recommendations and roadmap are entirely client-specific. The research determines WHAT we say; the MGS framework determines HOW and WHERE we say it. Never invent a different document structure per client, and never let discovered themes dictate document architecture — but never let the standardised structure produce standardised answers either.

## CLIENT-FACING WRITING DISCIPLINE

Layer 2 and Layer 3 must read as professional strategic analysis, not as an exposed evidence dossier. Do not repeatedly label claims FACT / LEADERSHIP INTENT / INTERNAL PERCEPTION / EXTERNAL OBSERVATION or STRONG / MODERATE evidence in the finished documents — that discipline should be visible through the quality and restraint of the writing, not through constant analytical tagging. Do not fill the finished strategy with "MGS Strategic Judgement Required," "Unsupported," "Open Item" or "Evidence Gap" markers unless the issue genuinely needs to be surfaced to the client for a decision only they can make; where something can be resolved through reasonable strategic judgement, resolve it and let MGS review the judgement call at the Human Review Gate rather than exporting the uncertainty into the client document.
