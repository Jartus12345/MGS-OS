# MGS Output Specification v1.0

## OUTPUT A — MGS MASTER CLIENT STRATEGY
The detailed strategic source of truth. Contains: business context; independent source findings; cross-analysis; strategic findings; central diagnosis; current vs desired position; perception gap; strategic opportunity; North Star; strategic priorities; 12-month perception goal; Build/Protect/Reduce; strategic pillars; Align/Execute/Improve roadmap; supporting evidence and reasoning. No fixed page count — depth follows evidence.

## OUTPUT B — CLIENT EXECUTIVE BRAND DIRECTION
Approximately 5–8 sections, for senior leadership approval. Structure:
1. Executive Context — why the exercise was undertaken.
2. Current Position — high-level findings from leadership, employees and external assessment.
3. Strategic Diagnosis — what has MGS concluded?
4. Strategic North Star — where should the organisation move?
5. 12-Month Desired Perception — what should change over the next year?
6. Strategic Priorities / Route Forward — how does MGS begin moving towards this position?
7. Conclusion / Alignment — what does approval unlock next?

This must be a genuine executive synthesis, not a truncation of the Master Strategy. Retain the most important strategic substance while removing unnecessary analytical depth.

## INTERNAL MGS OUTPUTS (preserved but not automatically client-facing)
Evidence Register; Source Analysis; Theme Matrix; Agreements; Contradiction Register; Perception Gaps; Hidden Strengths; Unsupported Ambitions; Underused Evidence; Current vs Desired Matrix; Strategic Findings; Evidence Gaps; Alignment Questions; Claim Audit; Genericness Audit; Reference Contamination Audit.
